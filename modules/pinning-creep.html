畴壁为什么不是一条匀速前进的直线？

Domain wall 一旦成为真正运动的自由度，下一个问题就不是“它能不能动”，而是：真实样品中的 quenched disorder 怎样让它停住、热激活地爬行、并在不同尺度上变粗糙。这个模块用三篇经典铁电工作把 pinning、creep、roughness 三种常被混用的语言拆开。

核心问题：看到迟滞、平台、非线性速度或毛糙畴壁时，分别能证明什么？什么时候还不能把现象叫作 depinning？

统一素材标准：三篇论文均以项目 Google Drive PDF 为权威版本；作者论述以可选择、可复制的 HTML 原文呈现；Figure 从对应 PDF 600 DPI 页面精裁为无损 PNG，不做有损压缩；点击图可查看原尺寸。

Pinning

局域势垒把墙困住；关心“在哪里停、什么缺陷造成”。

Creep

低驱动力下的热激活集体运动；速度对驱动力高度非线性。

Roughness

墙的几何涨落随观察尺度增长；用 B(L)、ζ 定量。

Depinning

真正的临界持续运动属于下一章，需要阈值与标度证据。

1 · Tybell 2002：先从速度律认出 creep

Tybell 等人用 PZT 薄膜中 AFM 写入畴的径向扩张提取 domain-wall speed。最关键的读图方式不是“速度变慢了”，而是看 ln v 对逆场的系统非线性：数据能用热激活 creep 形式描述，而且低场时速度指数级变小。

原文 · Tybell et al. 2002　Physical Review Letters 89, 097601 · published PDF pp.2–3 · 跨页恢复阅读顺序，原文未改

Figure 3 shows the wall speed as a function of the inverse field for three different film thicknesses. The data fits well to a creep formula, with μ = 1. The exact dynamical exponent μ is found to be 1.12, 1.01, and 1.21 for the 290, 370, and 810 Å thick films, respectively, with an estimated 10% rms error on the field.

Let us now consider the possible microscopic origins of the observed creep behavior. Creep phenomena are a consequence of competition between the elastic energy of a propagating interface, tending to keep it flat, and a pinning potential, preventing it from simply sliding when submitted to an external force. The dynamical exponent in the creep scenario depends on both the dimensionality of the system, and the nature of the pinning potential.

学习重点：creep 不是“墙走得慢”的同义词。作者明确把它写成 interface elasticity ↔ pinning potential 的竞争，并用驱动场依赖的速度律识别这一动力学区间。

这篇真正建立了什么

在这些 PZT 薄膜与实验时间尺度内，lateral DW motion 表现出 thermally activated creep，并把 μ 与界面维数/无序类型的理论语言接上。

不能直接推出什么

一条 creep 拟合并不能证明一个 depinning critical point，更不能把 μ 直接移植到 sliding FE；材料、界面弹性和 disorder 都不同。

速度律告诉我们“墙怎样慢慢走”，但还没有告诉我们墙本身长什么样。下一步把相机停下来：被无序扭曲后的静态墙，在不同长度尺度上到底有多粗糙？

2 · Paruch 2005：roughness 是一个尺度律，不是视觉形容词

Paruch 等人把畴壁边界写成位移函数 u(z)，再计算位移相关函数 B(L)。在 random-manifold 区间，若 B(L) ∝ L2ζ，那么 ζ 才是可比较的 roughness exponent。这里“墙看起来毛”完全不够；必须说明在哪个 L 区间拟合、斜率怎样得到 ζ。

原文 · Paruch et al. 2005　Physical Review Letters 94, 197601 · published PDF p.3 · 原文未改

From the power-law growth of B(L) at these short length scales, we extract a value for the roughness exponent ζ. This exponent characterizes the roughness of the domain wall in the random manifold regime where an interface individually optimizes its energy with respect to the disorder potential landscape and B(L) scales as B(L) ∝ L2ζ. As shown in Fig. 3(a), a linear fit of the lower part of the ln[B(L)] versus ln(L) curve allows 2ζ to be determined. Average values of ζ ≈ 0.26, 0.29, and 0.22 were obtained for the 50, 66, and 91 nm thick films, respectively, indicated by the horizontal lines in Fig. 3(b).

In addition to the investigations of static domain wall roughness described above, we independently measured domain wall dynamics in each film. As shown in Fig. 4, we observe the nonlinear velocity response to applied electric fields characteristic of a creep process.

这里把两类观测分得很干净：ζ 来自静态几何，creep exponent 来自动力学。两者都能约束 disorder/interface 模型，但不是同一个拟合量。
不要过度解读 ζ。 论文进一步用特定 disordered-elastic-system 理论讨论 random-bond / random-field 与有效维数；那一判断依赖弹性核和模型假设。教学上先保留最稳的结论：粗糙度是可量化的尺度相关几何，而不是“看着不直”。
Tybell 和 Paruch 给出的是“统计语言”。但真实器件里 pinning 到底长什么样？最后看一篇纳米电容实验：墙会在某个具体位置停住，随后在更强/更久的脉冲下继续走。

3 · Kim 2014：局域势垒可以直接留下“停住—再走”的痕迹

Kim 等人在 70 nm PZT nanocapacitor 中把 switched area 与脉冲时间一起看：曲线出现平台时，PFM 图里畴壁确实停在局域位置；随后继续增长对应 de-pinning。更有意思的是，他们看到两类 hindrance：一种像 point defect，另一种与 pristine domain structure 留下的局域缺陷分布相关。

原文 · Kim et al. 2014　Nano Convergence 1:24 · published PDF pp.1–3 · 跨页恢复阅读顺序，原文未改

The domain wall motion is occasionally hindered as shown in Figure 1(a). The normalized switched area temporarily remains constant at a value of around 0.5 [pinning: blue arrows of Figure 1(a)], and then starts to increase again [de-pinning: red arrows of Figure 1(a)] under the applied electric field. This pinning/de-pinning phenomenon was observed only for relatively low amplitude of the applied bias. It has been well known that the pinning procedure is dependent on local point defects, which can hinder the movement of a domain wall and could exist in principle anywhere inside the capacitors. However, if there is enough energy is provided to overcome the barrier height imposed by the defects, the domain wall can move through these defects, i.e., a de-pinning phenomenon occurs. As a result, the domain wall motion strongly depends on the correlation between the applied bias pulses and the barrier height of the local defect states.

However, we also observed a different type of domain wall hindrance as shown in Figure 1(c) for another nanocapacitor. In this case, intriguingly, the domain wall motion and the pinning seem to follow the pristine domain structure underlying the nanocapacitor. The domain wall is first pinned at the center of the capacitor, which is close to the domain boundary of the pristine domains. After the de-pinning, the right part of the domain wall moves and then the whole region of the capacitor is finally switched to a downward polarization. This domain wall propagation seems to have a particular relationship with the pristine domain structure. Even though there are pinning and de-pinning processes, pronounced bowing was not observed in both cases.

原文中的语法笔误也保留了，因为这里展示的是作者原句。物理上最重要的是：平台对应真实空间的停滞；提高驱动后墙继续传播；而 pinning source 不一定只有一种。

这篇增加了什么直觉

pinning landscape 不是抽象噪声：局域缺陷能让同一面墙出现停滞、局部先行和后续解钉，器件尺度越小，单个缺陷影响越显著。

为什么还不是下一章的 depinning

论文里的 “de-pinning” 是越过具体局域势垒的实验描述；它没有建立热力学极限下的临界阈值、β/ζ/ν 或 finite-size scaling，因此不能直接等同于 depinning universality。

这一章的最小判断框架：速度非线性 → 问是否 creep；形貌不直 → 测 B(L)、ζ；局域停滞 → 找 pinning landscape；看到一个阈值 → 先别叫 critical depinning。
