首页 / Domain Walls: What Actually Moves?

真正动的，到底是谁？

AB → BA 的示意图很容易让人脑补成“两整层一起横着滑”。但真正的问题不是能量面上有没有一条低势垒路径，而是：一个沿 z 方向的电场，凭什么在 x–y 平面启动这次运动？

这一页只做一件事：把“domain wall 很重要”这句空话拆开，追到三个可以检查的事实——完整单畴为什么难以被面外场横向推动；DW 为什么恰好能提供局域驱动力；实验又怎样看到墙被钉住、释放和继续移动。

1. 先把最容易混淆的两件事分开：低 barrier ≠ 电场能启动整体 sliding

早期 sliding-FE 图景里，常见做法是在势能面上人为沿某个面内方向移动一层，算出 AB → saddle point → BA 的 barrier。这个计算告诉你如果系统已经沿那条坐标走了，代价有多大；但它没有自动回答外加的面外电场怎样提供面内力。

原文 · Ke, Liu & Liu 2025PRL 135, 046201 · 正文第 2 页
We first establish that both the tensorial nature of BECs and the breaking of C3 symmetry underpin polarization reversal in sliding ferroelectrics. In the AB-stacked h-BN bilayer, the boron (B) atom in the upper layer is positioned directly above the nitrogen (N) atom in the lower layer, resulting in charge transfer that generates a polarization along the −z direction (P−), as illustrated in Fig. 1(a). In a simple point-charge model, where each atom is assigned a fixed charge, applying E3 generates forces solely in the out-of-plane direction, rendering in-plane atomic motion impossible. In contrast, the BEC directly reflects the force on atom κ in direction j induced by a macroscopic electric field in direction i. The off-diagonal elements of the BEC tensor are critical for generating in-plane atomic forces. However, the presence of C3 symmetry cancels these in-plane forces in both AB and BA configurations. For each unit cell, the net in-plane force can be approximated from the out-of-plane field and the off-diagonal BEC elements.
我会怎么读这一段

作者不是先说“DW 会动”，而是先找一个矛盾：外场是 z 向的，目标运动却在 x–y 面。BEC 是张量，因此原则上可以出现 Ez → Fx/y 的交叉响应；但在理想 AB、BA 单畴里，C₃ 对称性又把这些面内分量抵消了。这里真正卡住的是驱动力的对称性，不是势垒大小。

FIP ∼ Ez · Z*off-diagonal
这条式子要记什么？

不必背具体张量指标。记住一件事：面外场能不能驱动面内位移，取决于局域环境允许不允许相应的非对角响应留下净值。

所以 DW 为什么出现？

AB 到 BA 之间的墙本身就是 stacking 连续变化的区域。墙附近不再保持完整单畴的 C₃ 对称性，于是原本相消的面内力可以留下净值。墙因此不是“画在两个颜色之间的一条线”，而是外场真正能抓住的结构自由度。

2. Chen 2026 把这个机制画得很清楚：完整 domain 和 DW 的角色完全不同

看图顺序建议：先只看右上 (d)(e)，再看右中 (f)，最后才回头比较左侧传统铁电。这样不会被整张大图的信息量淹没。

(d) 先看力场

AB/BA 对应的稳定极化态处，面内净力是零；偏离这些高对称 stacking 后才出现明显的面内力矢量。也就是说，完整单畴不是“有个很小的力但没滑动”，而是理想对称状态本身没有启动整体横向运动的净驱动力。

(e) 再看 DW

把 AB 与 BA 接起来以后，红色的 Fy 只集中在墙附近。这个 panel 是整页最重要的证据：同一个面外场，对 domain interior 和 DW region 的作用并不等价。

(f) 才是“1D path”

这里的 1D 不是说材料是一维，而是说已有墙的推进把原本可在二维平面探索的切换过程压到一条受结构约束的传播方向上。

原文 · Chen et al. 2026PRX 16, 011066 · 正文第 3 页
This domain-nucleation-free property in sliding ferroelectrics allows the control of polarization switching to shift entirely to DW motion, thereby defining a new paradigm, “no domain wall, no polarization reversal,” that is fundamentally distinct from conventional ferroelectric switching. Interestingly, as shown in Fig. 1(f), DW motion is inherently restricted to a one-dimensional (1D) path within a two-dimensional (2D) atomic plane. This confinement significantly reduces the stochasticity observed in conventional ferroelectrics, where DW motion often follows complex pathways [Fig. 1(b)]. Moreover, the inevitable imperfect factors during device fabrication, such as bubbles, or edges of the sample, introduce additional energy barriers, further constraining DW motion. These factors ensure that the polarization reversal is dominated by predictable switching dynamics, enabling well-defined programmable states [Fig. 1(g)].
这里别只记那句 “no domain wall, no polarization reversal”

单独摘这句话会把作者论证截断。完整段落实际上同时说了三层东西：切换自由度从 nucleation 转移到已有 DW；墙的运动受到几何路径约束；真实器件里的 bubble / edge 又会给这条路径加上额外 barrier。第三层正是后面 pinning 章节的入口。

3. 机制不是只停在计算里：KPFM 直接看到了“在 domain 里加偏压”和“在墙上加偏压”的差别

原文 · Chen et al. 2026RESULTS · KPFM paragraph
To directly observe the DW motion at the microscopic scale, we conducted Kelvin probe force microscopy (KPFM) characterization. As shown in Fig. 2, clear domains with distinct surface potentials corresponding to AB- and BA-stacked configurations are observed, separated by narrow DWs. We applied a local electric field via the tip voltage to spatially control the polarization switching for tracking DW dynamics. By applying a positive tip voltage, the size of the BA domains increased, accompanied by a reduction in the size of the AB domains and the movement of the DW separating them. Notably, we exclude tip-induced artifacts in domain imaging and demonstrate that repeated dc bias within a single domain induces no DW motion, whereas a single dc bias at the wall readily drives domain expansion or contraction. These observations confirm that polarization switching is dominated by DW motion, thus motivating our focus on DW dynamics.
这个对照比“看到墙移动”更有信息量

同样是局域 dc bias，打在完整单畴里不触发 DW motion；打在已有墙的位置，却能直接让 domain expansion / contraction。 它把前面的 symmetry argument 和可观察的空间选择性接上了。

(a) → (b) → (c)

圆圈是 bubble。较低偏压时墙在 bubble 附近弯曲；提高偏压后墙跨过 bubble；再把偏压降回去，它仍继续移动并趋向更直的形貌。不要只看“颜色变了”，要盯着白色 DW 线和它相对 bubble 的位置。

(d) → (e) → (f)

粗糙边缘提供多个离散 pinning sites。墙的局部段依次脱钉，运动不是整条墙同时平移。这已经很接近“extended interface in a heterogeneous landscape”的语言。

原文 · Chen et al. 2026紧接 Fig. 2 的下一段，跨页
As shown in Fig. 2(a), after applying a 3.5-V probe voltage to the 3R-MoS2 sample, the movement of the DW was constrained upon encountering a bubble as a pinning site, resulting in localized deformation with inward curvature. However, when a higher voltage (5 V) was applied, the DW traversed the bubble, undergoing only slight deformation without splitting [Fig. 2(b)]. Upon reducing the voltage back to 3.5 V, the DW continued to move, and its morphology reverted to the expected straight configuration (the lowest-energy state), as shown in Fig. 2(c). We attribute this curvature to local pinning at the mesoscopic defect site, while the remaining segments of the domain wall retain nearly superlubric motion in the sliding ferroelectric. Notably, although lacking temporal resolution, KPFM unambiguously reveals nucleation through the spatial emergence of new domains. Indeed, no nucleation of oppositely polarized domains was observed throughout our measurements, even after multiple dc bias cycles applied within single-domain regions containing atomic-scale defects.
这一段把“pinning”说得比 caption 更完整

bubble 并没有把整条墙冻结：局部段被钉住，其他部分还能移动，所以墙先发生弯曲；驱动再增大时才越过障碍。这种“弹性代价 + 局域 pinning + 外驱动”的竞争，就是下一章 creep / depinning 理论会抽象出来的东西。

4. Liu 2026 提供了另一种、独立的结构读出：Raman 看到真实 multilayer device 不是一起切换

Chen 的 KPFM 很适合看局部墙怎么动；Liu 的 shear-mode Raman imaging 则更适合回答器件尺度上“不同区域是不是走同一条路径”。这里最值得保留的不是一句“pinning strongly influences the dynamics”，而是它前后完整的实验逻辑。

原文 · Liu et al. 2026PRL 136, 206202 · Fig. 2 discussion
We tracked the domain-switching process using Raman mapping of the shear-mode frequency. Figure 2(c) shows the optical micrograph of a trilayer flake exfoliated on a SiO2/Si substrate, and Fig. 2(d) presents the corresponding Raman map acquired immediately after the flake was integrated into Device D35. Guided by the stacking-dependent shear-mode frequencies discussed above, we identified predominantly ABC or CBA stacking across the flake, with small ABA or BAB domains located near the lower-right corner. The presence of multiple domains in as-fabricated devices is essential for ferroelectric switching; devices with a single domain showed no switching within our field window. This is consistent with the theoretical expectation that in the absence of DWs, an out-of-plane field generates no in-plane driving force for interlayer sliding.
为什么这段值得和 Chen / Ke 放在一起

它不是同一组作者用另一种措辞重复理论，而是用 stacking-sensitive Raman 给出独立的器件尺度观察：实验场窗内，已有 multiple domains 的器件能切换，而 single-domain device 没有看到切换。

原文 · Liu et al. 2026同一页，Region 2 discussion
At first glance, the switching behavior in Region 2 appears different from that in Region 1, as suggested by the Raman maps [Fig. 2(e)] and field-dependent Raman spectra at a given location [Fig. 2(g)]. However, because the switching in Region 2 was also reproducible, the same CBA ↔ ABA (or BAB) ↔ ABC pathway can be inferred, and the difference from Region 1 lies in the dwell time of intermediate states. In Region 2, the field at which the intermediate state emerged in the backward scan in Figs. 2(e) and 2(g) differs by ∼0.10 V/nm. Such variations in the apparent critical field were frequently observed, indicating that microscopic details of the switching can vary between cycles due to pinning by defects.
这里终于能谨慎地谈“critical field 会漂”

作者说的是 apparent critical field，并把循环间约 0.10 V/nm 的变化联系到 defect pinning。它提醒我们：器件 hysteresis 里读出来的一个 switching field，未必就是某个纯净体系唯一的“本征材料常数”。

5. 到这里，哪些结论已经够硬，哪些还不能往前多说一步？

现在可以比较有把握地说

在 3R-MoS₂ 等代表性 sliding ferroelectric 中，pre-existing DW 可以成为 polarization reversal 的主导自由度；DW region 的局域 symmetry breaking 允许面外场产生面内驱动力；bubble、rough edge 和 microscopic defects 会局域钉扎墙，并改变实际 switching pathway 与 apparent threshold。

现在还不能直接说

这些结果本身没有证明 sliding-FE DW 属于某个已知 depinning universality class，也没有给出一组 universal exponents。看到 pinning、threshold、hysteresis，甚至某段 power law，都不等于完成了 universality identification。

所以从这一章走到下一章，问题会自然变成：既然墙是一条有内部弹性的 extended object，而缺陷给它一个空间不均匀的 landscape，那么低驱动下它怎么 creep？接近 threshold 时怎么 depin？形貌粗糙度和速度又该怎么标度？
