# Current Frontiers lesson provenance

Module 08 is intentionally a synthesis/evidence-audit page rather than another set of duplicated paper assets.

## Source policy

All source passages in `modules/current-frontiers.html` were checked against PDFs inside the project Google Drive folder `sliding ferroelectric/` and its literature subfolders. Source text is selectable HTML; no paragraph screenshots are used.

Core papers audited:

- Jing Liang et al. (2025), *Resolving polarization switching pathways of sliding ferroelectricity in trilayer 3R-MoS2*.
- Ziwen Wang & Shuai Dong (2025), *Polarization switching in sliding ferroelectrics: Roles of fluctuation and domain wall*.
- Changming Ke, Fucai Liu & Shi Liu (2025), *Superlubric Motion of Wavelike Domain Walls in Sliding Ferroelectrics*.
- Jiangang Chen et al. (2026), *Deterministic 1D Domain Wall Motion with Nucleation-Free Nature in Sliding Ferroelectric Switching*.
- Yulu Liu et al. (2026), *Shear-Mode Raman Imaging of Ferroelectric Switching in Multilayer 3R-MoS2*.

## Interpretation boundary

The evidence scorecard distinguishes direct observations from interpretation. In particular, terms such as `avalanchelike`, `superlubric`, `pinning/depinning event`, and `critical depinning universality` are not treated as synonyms. The absence of time-resolved constant-field `v(E)`, roughness exponents, correlation-length scaling, and finite-size scaling is explicitly recorded rather than inferred away.
