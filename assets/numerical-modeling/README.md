# Numerical Modeling lesson assets

Sources are restricted to the project Google Drive folder `sliding ferroelectric/` and its literature subfolders.

## Evidence policy

- Source passages in `modules/numerical-modeling.html` are selectable HTML text, not paragraph screenshots.
- Figures are frozen local PNG assets taken from the authoritative Drive PDFs. No scientific annotations were added.
- Chen Fig. 6 is the PDF's own embedded 396×86 raster object and is intentionally not upscaled.
- Caballero Fig. 1 and the two teaching crops from Fig. 5 preserve the original crop, axes, legends, curve ordering and line-style information. They are grayscale indexed PNGs to keep the frozen scientific figure readable while staying small enough for static delivery; no scientific annotation or geometric redrawing was added.
- The white-noise grid-scaling section is explicitly labeled as a site derivation, not a quotation or claim attributed to the papers.
